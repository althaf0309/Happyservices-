# Interim stock photos

These are stand-ins until Happy Services supplies its own photography. All are from Unsplash under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no attribution required, but credited here anyway.

They are loaded with `python manage.py load_stock_photos`. The command only fills empty slots, so photos uploaded in the admin always win. Re-run it with `--replace-stock` to refresh stock photos; client uploads are never touched.

Choices made on purpose:

- **The home "Why Happy Services" slot has no person in it.** The design asks for the client's real technician there, so a stock photo of a stranger would imply that person is on their team.
- **No photo shows a visible appliance brand logo**, in line with the handoff's "no third-party logos" rule.
- **Region photos are verified locations.** Unsplash records each one as taken in that district.
